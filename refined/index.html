When several companies, jurisdictions and advisers are involved, responsibilities tend to blur and good advice can stall somewhere between one professional and the next. Decisions become harder to make, and harder still to carry through.

Clarendon Advisory helps business owners, directors and family offices see clearly how their structure works, where the gaps are and what needs to happen next. We then stay involved, working alongside your lawyers and accountants, until the agreed actions have been completed.

When to involve Clarendon

Clients usually come to us when one or more of the following applies:

Our job is to bring the whole situation into view and agree a sensible way forward.

  • Several jurisdictionsSeveral companies or jurisdictions are involved, and a decision in one place affects another.
  • Unclear authorityIt is no longer clear who is responsible for what, or who has authority to decide.
  • Connected issuesLegal, accounting and operational issues need to be looked at together rather than in isolation.
  • One clear pictureDirectors or principals need a single, clear picture of what is still outstanding.
  • Change and transitionA reorganisation, change of ownership or change of adviser needs someone to see it through.
  • DisputesA dispute raises governance questions alongside the legal work.
  • Advice not yet implementedAdvice has been given, but no one has yet taken charge of putting it into practice.

Our approach

More than coordination

Specialist advisers are experts in their own field. Advice given in one area, however, often has consequences in another, and those consequences can easily fall through the gaps.

That is where we add most value. We look at how the pieces connect, check that responsibilities are clear, raise the questions that have not yet been asked and help clients understand the decisions in front of them. Coordinating advisers is part of the job, but the real value lies in understanding the substance and keeping continuity from the first review through to implementation.

Outcomes

What the work delivers

A clear picture

We bring together information that usually sits with different people and advisers, so that you can see the entities, the arrangements, the open issues and how they connect.

A practical plan

A plan setting out what needs attention, which points need specialist advice and who is responsible for each action.

Governance that fits

Decision-making, reporting and controls that suit the way the business actually runs, without unnecessary bureaucracy.

Follow-through

We keep track of agreed actions and open questions, and make sure that decisions are reflected in the company records and in day-to-day practice.

For advisers and principals

Working with professional advisers

We work alongside lawyers, accountants and other specialists on matters that call for governance and operational judgement across several strands of work. We help organise the wider picture, identify missing information and put agreed recommendations into effect. Each adviser remains responsible for their own specialist advice.

For principals and family offices, this brings continuity. For professional advisers, it brings an experienced counterpart who understands how the pieces fit together and sees the work through.

Process

How we work

  1. 01

    Understand the matter

    We talk through the circumstances, the parties involved and the support you need. We carry out our due diligence and agree the scope, responsibilities and fees in writing.

  2. 02

    Assess the position

    We review the relevant information, governance arrangements and existing advice, and identify gaps, connections and questions that need further work.

  3. 03

    Agree the plan

    We set out priorities, decision points and responsibilities, and identify where further specialist advice is needed.

  4. 04

    Carry the work forward

    We work with you and your advisers to move agreed actions forward, deal with obstacles and keep clear records.

  5. 05

    Ongoing support

    Where needed, we continue to support governance and operations under an agreed scope of work.
