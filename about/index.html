About

Experience across governance, risk and implementation

Our role is to understand the wider situation, identify the gaps and connections, and help clients agree and carry through the right course of action.

Clarendon Advisory was set up to meet a need we saw again and again: senior involvement that connects corporate governance, commercial judgement and the practical work of putting decisions into effect.

Our founder

Asaf Almagor

Founder · Governance, Risk and Operational Advisory

Asaf has held senior governance, risk and operational roles in family office and international business environments, working directly with principals, directors and professional advisers.

His experience includes designing governance frameworks, developing reporting and control arrangements, assessing operational risk and supporting work across interconnected corporate structures.

He has worked with international lawyers, accountants, banks, trustees and corporate service providers, bringing their contributions together with a focus on governance, accountability and implementation.

Asaf holds an LLB and an MBA in International and Business Taxation, and has completed his studies for the ICA Diploma in Governance, Risk and Compliance. During his previous family office employment in Switzerland, he undertook mandatory annual VQF training.

Every Clarendon engagement is led by Asaf personally.
