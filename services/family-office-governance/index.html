Family Office Governance and Operational Risk

A clear view across family office structures

We help principals and their teams build a single, clear understanding of governance and operational risk across the structure.

Family offices often involve several companies, investment holding structures, professional advisers and operating responsibilities. As arrangements evolve, information and accountability can become spread thin.

Find out about us

How we help

Discuss family office governance

  • Reviewing responsibilities across companies and service providers.

  • Putting governance frameworks and reporting arrangements in place.

  • Identifying operational dependencies and gaps in controls.

  • Developing risk registers and escalation procedures.

  • Clarifying the scope, accountability and reporting of external advisers.

  • Supporting continuity when the organisation or its service providers change.

Outcomes

What you receive

Depending on the engagement, you may receive a governance framework, a map of responsibilities, a reporting schedule, a risk register and a prioritised improvement plan.

Our focus is on how the structure is governed and run. Specialist advice comes from the relevant appointed professionals.
