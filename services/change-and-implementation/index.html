Corporate Change and Implementation

Making corporate change work across the whole structure

We help clients understand these knock-on effects and carry the agreed changes through.

A reorganisation or change of ownership creates work well beyond the main transaction. Responsibilities, reporting, records, service provider arrangements and day-to-day practice may all need attention.

How we help

Discuss a programme of change

  • Assessing the governance and operational effects across the companies involved.

  • Bringing specialist recommendations together into one implementation plan.

  • Clarifying decision points, responsibilities and deadlines.

  • Supporting changes of adviser and service provider.

  • Tracking outstanding information, documents and actions.

  • Checking that agreed changes are reflected in how things actually work.

What you receive

An engagement may include an implementation plan, a responsibility tracker, progress reports and a record of matters still needing action.

We keep the different strands of work joined up and help deal with obstacles as they arise.
