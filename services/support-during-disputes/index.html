Governance Support Alongside Disputes and Legal Proceedings

Keeping governance and operations in order during a dispute

A dispute or other significant corporate matter can raise immediate questions about authority, information, reporting and continuity.

Alongside the legal work, directors and principals often need a clear view of the structure and practical help with the governance and operational issues that arise. We provide that support together with the client's legal counsel and other advisers.

Find out about us

How we help

Discuss your requirements

  • Setting out the relevant company relationships and governance responsibilities.

  • Identifying missing information and decisions still to be made.

  • Assessing operational dependencies and what is needed to keep things running.

  • Organising the governance and operational work that follows from specialist advice.

  • Keeping clear records of actions and responsibilities.

  • Supporting implementation as the matter develops.

Outcomes

What you receive

Depending on the circumstances, you may receive a map of governance issues, a list of information requirements, a prioritised action plan and ongoing progress reports.

Legal strategy and legal advice remain with the client's appointed counsel.
