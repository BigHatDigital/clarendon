Corporate Governance and Structure Reviews

Understand how your corporate structure works in practice

We review how the companies, ownership, governance and operations connect, identify the gaps and agree practical improvements.

A structure can be well documented and still leave important questions unanswered: who can take a decision, who is accountable, how information is reported, and whether the arrangements still suit the business as it changes.

Find out about us

When this helps

This work is useful when a business has grown across several companies, when existing arrangements have become hard to follow, when responsibilities have become fragmented, or when a clear starting point is needed before a change.

Discuss a governance review

What we look at

  • How the companies relate to each other, and the ownership information available.

  • Board processes, delegated authority and who makes which decisions.

  • Reporting lines and how information flows.

  • Company records and any documentation that is missing or out of date.

  • Connections between companies, advisers and day-to-day operations.

  • Issues that need legal, tax, accounting or other specialist advice.

Outcomes

What you receive

Depending on the scope, you may receive a structure overview, our governance findings, a prioritised action plan and recommendations on reporting, responsibilities and controls.

We can also help put the agreed improvements into place.
