Board and Director Governance Support

Better information for better decisions

Directors need to understand the matters in front of them, the information behind each decision and the responsibilities that follow from it.

We help boards and individual directors organise governance issues, identify open questions and put practical oversight arrangements in place.

Find out about us

How we help

Discuss board or director support

  • Reviewing the information and governance context around a decision.

  • Identifying unclear responsibilities and gaps in reporting.

  • Developing board processes, delegated authority and escalation arrangements.

  • Organising matters that need specialist professional advice.

  • Supporting company records and following up agreed actions.

  • Working with legal advisers and insurance professionals on directors' responsibilities, indemnities and insurance.

Outcomes

What you receive

An engagement may produce an assessment of governance issues, records of decisions and actions, a reporting framework or a plan for improving board processes.

The aim is to give directors a sound basis for oversight and to make sure agreed work gets done.
